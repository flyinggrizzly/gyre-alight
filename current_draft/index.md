# Gyre Alight

_Gyre Alight_ is an a crude hack of _Torchbearer 2e_.

## Goal

_Gyre Alight_ is an attempt to get Players to choose, when delving deep into a dungeon[^1], whether they will

- **build Momentum so they can tackle the greatest challenges, while accepting the greatest risks**
- **rest to recover, and in so doing bleed Momentum, but also avoiding the greatest risks**

I'm trying to get players to a space where they are making **an exciting, risky, and nail-biting choice** about swinging
for the fences, vs letting out the steamhead so they can incrementally delve longer.

These are some basic rules, hopefully just enough of a framework to allow playtesting of the Momentum mechanic. They are
adapted from _Torchbearer 2e_ by Thor Olavsrud and Luke Crane.
